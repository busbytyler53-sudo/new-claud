The name

Six letters. Three syllables. Shortens to the sun.

Solano is the old name for a hot summer wind that blows in off the Mediterranean. It sounds Italian, it's easy to say in any accent, and it looks good spaced out in capitals, which matters more than people think when it's stitched on a chest.

It also shortens on its own. Nobody has to be told to call it Sol. That's the trick Amici and Culture Club both pull: a name that feels like membership, and a nickname the regulars use. Sol is also Latin for sun, so the short name and the tropical drops tell the same story.

The short name

Sol. for the drops, the S for everything small.

Bodoni italic with a full stop. The full stop is what makes it a name and not just the word for sun. It sits small on the front of every drop tee so the back print does the talking.

The italic S in a thin ring. It's the sun, and it's the seal on the box. Caps, buttons, zip pulls, the profile picture. Works down to about 10mm.

The core range

Plain on purpose.

This is most of what you sell, all year. Heavy cotton, good colours, a small logo and nothing else. The core sets the price point and the reputation. Get the fabric and fit right here and the drops sell themselves.

The drops

Three summer tees. Small run each, then gone.

The drops are where the brand gets to be loud, and only on the back. Each one is named after a place on the Italian coast and printed in limited numbers. Order 150 to 300 per design for the first season, and when one sells out, don't restock it. Scarcity is what makes the next drop sell.

Before you print anything

The boring part that saves the brand.

  1. Run a class 25 (clothing) trademark search for SOLANO with the USPTO, UKIPO and EUIPO. A web search turned up no clothing label using it, but Solano is also a surname and a California county, so expect other businesses with the name. You want clothing specifically to be clear.
  2. Grab the domain and handles the same day. If @solano is gone, @solano.club reads well and matches the membership feel.
  3. Print one sample of each drop tee before ordering the run. Screen print the back graphics with 4 to 5 colours each, and check the thin palm leaflets hold up on the screen mesh.
  4. Get a stitch-out of the script mark and the sun monogram at real size. Bodoni hairlines need thickening for embroidery under about 4cm.
