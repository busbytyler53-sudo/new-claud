Ospiti · Blanks, made properly

The idea

Fewer things. Better made. Every colour goes with every other.

The look is blanks. Heavy cotton, boxy cuts, muted colours, and a logo so small and tonal you only see it up close. It's the stuff everyone wears and nobody gets bored of, which is why it sells season after season without markdowns.

Agosto still carries the story underneath: the Italian August, the swallow that comes back every summer. But the story is whispered now. It lives in the colour names, the AGO. under the back collar and the patch inside the hoodie. The front of the garment stays quiet.

Ospiti · the core

One tee, six colours.

Same pattern, same weight, six neutrals. Wordmark across the chest in a tone one shade darker than the cloth. Launch with these six and add one colour a season, never more.

The details

The story is on the inside.

No big graphics on the core range. The brand shows up where people who own it will notice: the rubber patch on the back neck of every fleece, AGO. printed under the collar, the swallow on the cap.

Souvenir · summer only

The tropical drops, done tonal.

The lemons, the palms and the swallows stay, printed one shade off the shirt colour so they read as texture from across a room and detail up close. Clean enough to sit next to the blanks, different enough to sell out. Small runs, one a month through summer, never restocked.

Marks

Five marks, all quiet.

One wide sans for everything. No serifs, no script. The swallow is the only drawing in the system.

Colour

Six neutrals, Italian names.

Every colour is named in Italian, which is the cheapest way to keep the story alive on a product page. The logo is always the same colour as the garment, one tone darker, or one tone lighter on navy and black.

Before you order

Do these first.

  1. Get a class 25 trademark search done for AGOSTO in the countries you'll sell in. A web search found nothing in clothing, but that's not clearance.
  2. Order fabric swatches in all six colours and check them in daylight. Oat and stone look identical under shop lights at some mills.
  3. Make one sample tee and one sample hoodie, wash them three times, and check shrinkage and twist before committing to a run.
  4. Test the tonal print on one blank. If the contrast is too low on Latte, push the ink a shade darker; the files make that a one-line change.
