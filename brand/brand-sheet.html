Goes where you go

The story

The warm wind that goes everywhere.

Solano is the old name for a warm summer wind that blows in off the Mediterranean. It doesn't stay in one place. It moves through the beach in the morning, the town at midday, the terrace at night. Same wind, all day, everywhere.

That's the brand. Clothes that go where you go: the run, the office, the coffee, the dinner, the late walk home. Same palette, same clean cuts, same quiet branding, so the training short works with the knit polo and the evening shirt works with the hoodie. Pick something up every day and it never gets old, because it was never trying to be loud.

And the name shortens on its own. Sol is the sun. Nobody has to be told to call it that.

The names

Solano, Sol. and the Solano Club.

A brand people talk about gets said in different ways. Solano gives you three, plus a mark that needs no words.

SOL. The short name. What regulars call it, and what goes on sleeves, back necks and the handle: @sol.solano.

Sun and wind. A sun with the wind running through it. The emblem for caps, socks and activewear. Once people know it, it replaces the name.

Solano Club. The community. A Sunday run club, the people who wear it, the drops they get first. This is your Culture Club: membership, not merch.

The lines

Four lines, one look.

Each line is a part of the day, so the range explains itself on the website. Same colours and the same branding rules across all four, which is what stops it feeling like a brand with no niche.

The base

One tee, six colours, under everything.

The heavyweight tee sits across all four lines: under the overshirt, under the hoodie, on its own at the gym. It's the piece people buy first and buy again. Tonal wordmark, one shade off the cloth.

Marks

Six marks, all quiet.

One wide sans for everything. The sun and wind is the only drawing in the system. Branding gets quieter as the day goes on: bold on Strada, small on Sport, label only on Giorno and Sera.

Colour

Six colours. Everything goes with everything.

The same six colours across all four lines, so a customer can mix a Sport short with a Strada hoodie and a Giorno polo and it always works. The logo is the garment colour, one shade darker, or one shade lighter on Olive, Navy and Black.

Before you order

Do these first.

  1. Get a class 25 trademark search done for SOLANO. A web search found no clothing label using it, but it is also a surname and a California county, so expect other businesses with the name.
  2. Grab the handles and domain the same day: @solano, @solano.club, @sol.solano, solano.club.
  3. Start the Solano Club before the shop opens: a free Sunday run, a group chat, first access to drops. A hundred regulars wearing it every week is worth more than any ad.
  4. Activewear is a different factory from cut-and-sew basics. Find a separate supplier for Sport and test stretch, squat-proofing and sweat-wicking before launch.
  5. Sample everything, wash it three times, and check shrinkage before committing to a run.
