The story

Nine to nine. One wardrobe for the whole day.

Nove is Italian for nine. 09:00 is the gym, the commute, the first coffee. 21:00 is the table for two, the bar, the walk home. Everything in between is a normal day, and most people own three wardrobes to get through it: gym kit that looks like gym kit, work clothes that look like work, and one good shirt for going out.

Nove is one wardrobe that does all of it. Same palette, same clean cuts, same quiet branding, so the training short works with the knit polo and the evening shirt works with the hoodie. You don't have to think about it. You just get dressed.

That's the whole idea, and it's why the brand fits activewear, smart casual, date night and streetwear without feeling like four different labels.

The names

Nove, N°9 and a clock that always says nine.

Four ways the brand gets said and seen. Customers will shorten it on their own, and the clock means the logo works with no words at all.

N°9. The short name. Sleeves, back necks, the handle (@nove.n9). Reads in English, Italian and French.

The clock. Hands at nine o'clock. The emblem for caps, socks, buttons and the activewear. Once people know it, it replaces the name.

09:00 / 21:00. The story in two numbers. Back prints, packaging, the hang tag, the bottom of every email.

The lines

Four lines, one look.

Each line is named after a time of day, so the range explains itself on the website. Same colours and the same branding rules across all four, which is what stops it feeling like a brand with no niche. The niche is the whole day.

Activewear

Training tees, shorts and layers that go from the gym straight into the day. Quick-dry, four-way stretch, cut like the rest of the range so nothing looks like gym kit at the coffee shop.

Streetwear

The base

One tee, six colours, under everything.

The heavyweight tee sits across all four lines: under the overshirt, under the hoodie, on its own at the gym. It's the piece people buy first and buy again. Tonal wordmark, one shade off the cloth.

Six marks, all quiet.

One wide sans for everything. The clock is the only drawing in the system. Branding gets quieter as the day goes on: bold on Strada, small on Sport, label only on Giorno and Sera.

Before you order

Do these first.

  1. Get a class 25 trademark search done for NOVE. Earlier US filings for it in clothing were cancelled or abandoned, which is a good sign, but get it confirmed properly before you spend.
  2. Grab the handles and domain the same day: @nove, @nove.n9, @novestudio, nove.studio.
  3. Activewear is a different factory from cut-and-sew basics. Find a separate supplier for Sport and test stretch, squat-proofing and sweat-wicking before launch.
  4. Sample everything, wash it three times, and check shrinkage before committing to a run.
