Built to stay · Year 01

The story

Everyone owns one piece that never left.

The grey sweatshirt you've had since school. The tee that's been through every holiday, every move, every version of you. It's faded, it's softer than anything you've bought since, and you still reach for it first. That piece has tenure. It earned its place by staying.

Tenure makes that piece on purpose. Heavy cotton, cuts that don't date, colours that go with each other and with everything else you own. No trends to chase, nothing loud to get bored of. Clothes for the actual week you live: the gym at 6, the laptop at 9, the train home, the late dinner, the Sunday that goes nowhere.

Every piece is labelled with the year it was made. The idea is simple: the older your label, the better it looks on you.

The names

One brand, four ways to say it.

Strong brands get used in different ways by different people. Customers shorten it, the press writes it out in full, the logo works without words. Tenure is built to be said all four ways.

TenureThe full nameWhat's on the shop front, the website and the press. Sounds established on day one.
TNR.The short nameWhat the regulars call it, and the handle: @tnr.studio. Goes on sleeves and under collars.
The tallyThe markFive strokes, one for each year. Counting time is the whole idea. Once people know it, it replaces the name.
Year 01The collectionEvery collection is a year, not a season. Year 01, Year 02. Owners collect the labels.

The core

One tee, six colours.

Same pattern, same weight, six neutrals. Wordmark across the chest in a tone one shade off the cloth, so it's there when you look for it and invisible when you don't. Launch with six and add one colour a year, never more.

{{BLANKS}}

The year label

Every piece is numbered.

This is the detail that makes a blanks brand feel like more than blanks.

{{LABEL}}

A woven label inside the back neck carries the year and a number. The first thousand hoodies are N° 0001 to N° 1000 of Year 01, and that never happens again.

It gives early customers a reason to buy now, it gives you a story every launch, and in five years someone will post their faded Year 01 hoodie and that post will do more for the brand than any ad.

The details

Quiet on the outside.

No big graphics. The brand shows up where owners notice: the year label, TNR. under the collar, the tally on the cap.

{{SWATCHES}}

Before you order

Do these first.

  1. Get a class 25 trademark search done for TENURE in the countries you'll sell in. A web search found no clothing label using it (the nearest is Ten C, an Italian outerwear brand), but that isn't clearance.
  2. Grab the handles the same day: @tenure, @tnr.studio, @tenure.studio, and the domain.
  3. Make the story yours. If you have a real piece that's lasted, your dad's sweatshirt or your first good hoodie, photograph it and use it in the launch. A real one beats the line on this page.
  4. Sample one tee and one hoodie, wash them three times, and check shrinkage and twist before committing to a run.
