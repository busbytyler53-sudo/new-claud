The story

Born of the Italian summer. Made for every day.

Solano is the old Mediterranean name for the warm wind that comes in off the sea in summer. Along the Italian coast it's the wind of long days: espresso at the bar in the morning, the walk along the water at midday, the terrace at night. It doesn't stay in one place. It goes everywhere, all day.

That's the brand. Italian in its roots, clean in how it looks, and made to go where you go: the run, the office, the coffee, the dinner, the walk home. Six colours with Italian names, cuts that don't date, branding that whispers. Pick it up every day and it never gets old, because it was never trying to be loud.

The name shortens on its own. Sol is the sun. And the line on every label says it in Italian first: va dove vai, it goes where you go.

The names

Solano, Sol. and the Solano Club.

The heritage lives in the serif wordmark, the crest and the Italian on every label. Everything around it stays clean.

The lines

Four lines, one look.

Each line is a part of the day, so the range explains itself on the website. Same colours and the same branding rules across all four, which is what stops it feeling like a brand with no niche.

The base

One tee, six colours, under everything.

The heavyweight tee sits across all four lines: under the overshirt, under the hoodie, on its own at the gym. It's the piece people buy first and buy again. Tonal wordmark, one shade off the cloth. Colours named in Italian on every tag.

Marks

Heritage in the type. Clean everywhere else.

Bodoni for the name, a wide sans for everything else. The sun and wind and the crest are the only drawings in the system. Branding gets quieter as the day goes on: bold on Strada, small on Sport, label only on Giorno and Sera.

Colour

Sei colori. Everything goes with everything.

The same six colours across all four lines, so a customer can mix a Sport short with a Strada hoodie and a Giorno polo and it always works. The logo is the garment colour, one shade darker, or one shade lighter on Oliva, Notte and Nero.

{{SWATCHES}}

Before you order

Do these first.

  1. Get a class 25 trademark search done for SOLANO. A web search found no clothing label using it, but it is also a surname and a California county, so expect other businesses with the name.
  2. Grab the handles and domain the same day: @solano, @solano.club, @sol.solano, solano.club.
  3. Start the Solano Club before the shop opens: a free Sunday run, a group chat, first access to drops. A hundred regulars wearing it every week is worth more than any ad.
  4. Activewear is a different factory from cut-and-sew basics. Find a separate supplier for Sport and test stretch, squat-proofing and sweat-wicking before launch.
  5. Sample everything, wash it three times, and check shrinkage before committing to a run.
